HOW A SERVICE NIGHT WORKS

Out by 8 PM. Gone by morning. Reported by Monday.

  1. 1

    Residents set out trash

    Bagged and tied, in the CBES container, by 8 PM.

  2. 2

    Our team arrives

    Uniformed valets led by an Area Lead clock in on site.

  3. 3

    Door-to-door pickup

    Every door, every building. Issues logged with a photo.

  4. 4

    Trash goes to your on-site dumpster; we check the area before leaving.

  5. 5

    Closed out and reported

    The night rolls into your Monday report.
