The rules, in plain language

  1. 1

    Bag it and tie it. Loose trash can't be collected.

  2. 2

    Container out by 8 PM, Sunday to Thursday.

  3. 3

    Bags fit inside with the lid closed. Nothing next to the container or in the breezeway.

  4. 4

    Break down boxes and take them to the trash area yourself.

  5. 5

    No hazardous items, liquids or furniture at the door. Book a bulk pickup instead.

  6. 6

    Bring the container in by [10 AM] the next morning.

What happens if trash doesn't follow the rules?

Our valet logs it with a photo of the trash and doorway and shares it with your property management office, which handles any notice. CBES never fines residents.
